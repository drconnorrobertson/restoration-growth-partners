What These Case Studies Show

Every restoration company is different, but these case studies illustrate three key principles that work consistently:

Diversification Works

Companies that build multiple lead sources grow faster and more sustainably than those dependent on any single channel. Combining paid ads, organic search, and relationship-based leads creates resilience.

Systems Scale

When you build systems instead of relying on random leads, growth becomes predictable. You can hire confidently, plan expansion, and forecast revenue accurately.

Margins Improve

The biggest win isn't just more leads. It's keeping more of the revenue you generate. Reducing marketplace dependency from 25% commission to 5-10% on owned channels dramatically improves profitability.
