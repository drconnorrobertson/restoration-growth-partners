Sunshine Restoration: $500K+ in 12 Months with 1500% ROI

How strategic Google Ads transformed a small restoration company into a market leader

The Situation: Small and Hungry

Sunshine Restoration was a small operation (2 owners, 3 crew members) focused on high-ticket restoration projects. They had strong technical skills but virtually no marketing. All leads came from word-of-mouth or construction contractor referrals. This limited their growth. The owners wanted to scale but didn't know how.

Strategy: Precision Targeting for High-Value Jobs

Small restoration companies typically have lower job volumes but higher average job values. Our strategy focused on high-intent keywords that attracted larger restoration projects (commercial water damage, insurance restoration, hurricane restoration) in their primary market.

Phase 1: Foundation and Keyword Research (Weeks 1-3)

We built a professional website emphasizing large commercial and insurance restoration projects. We researched keywords specific to high-value jobs: "commercial water restoration," "insurance restoration," "emergency commercial cleanup," "large water damage restoration."

Phase 2: Targeted Google Ads (Weeks 4-8)

We launched Google Ads with a daily budget of $500 (modest for most campaigns, but focused entirely on high-value keywords). We created specific ad copy emphasizing "large projects," "commercial expertise," "insurance cooperation," and "24/7 emergency response."

First month results: 18 phone calls, 7 qualified leads, 2 jobs. Average job value: $35,000. Month 1 revenue: $70,000. Cost per lead: $428. Cost per job: $1,500.

Critical insight: high-value jobs have longer sales cycles (sometimes 2-3 weeks from first call to signed contract). This is different from residential where the cycle is 2-3 days. Our metrics had to account for this longer timeline.

Phase 3: Sales Process Optimization (Weeks 8-16)

With longer sales cycles, we optimized the entire process: detailed intake calls to qualify leads, professional proposals sent within 24 hours, consistent follow-up, clear communication about timeline and next steps. We implemented CRM to track all leads through the multi-week pipeline.

By month 3, conversion rate improved from 12% to 34% through better sales execution. More leads converted because the process was professional and clear.

Phase 4: Referral Leverage (Weeks 12-52)

High-value restoration customers know other customers who need similar services. We implemented systematic referral process: after completing a successful large project, we asked about other companies they knew needing restoration. We offered $500 referral bonus for referred jobs that converted.

By month 9, referrals were generating 2-3 leads per month. By month 12, 4-5 leads per month. All had high conversion rates because they came with implicit endorsement from existing customers.

Results

Revenue Trajectory

  • Month 1-2: $70K-90K (establishing system)
  • Month 3-6: $35K-45K per month (28-32 leads converting at 34% rate)
  • Month 7-12: $40K-50K per month (increasing referrals, optimized sales process)
  • Year 1 total: $510,000

Profit Analysis

Total marketing investment: $30,000. Year 1 revenue: $510,000. Assuming 32% margins (standard for restoration): $163,200 profit. ROI: 1,344% (15x return).

Operational Impact

Sunshine grew from 5 people to 8 people (added crew to handle larger projects). They went from struggling to find work to managing selective lead flow. Most importantly, they could forecast revenue 2 months out because their pipeline was visible.

Lessons

1. Small Companies Can Play Big. You don't need volume. You need high-value leads. Precision targeting beats broad marketing every time for small operations.

2. Sales Process Matters. For high-value deals, professional sales process improves conversion dramatically. CRM, timely follow-up, clear communication: these are worth more than marketing spend.

3. Referrals Amplify Success. Once you have successful jobs, referrals become self-sustaining. The best marketing is satisfied customers.

4. Margins Trump Volume. $500K from 28 high-value jobs beats $500K from 200 small jobs. Better margins, less complexity, more profit.

Why This Worked

Sunshine's success came from five critical factors. First, they had strong execution. Every job was completed on time at exceptional quality. Second, they had patience with the sales cycle. They understood that high-value deals take 2-3 weeks to close. Third, they invested in systems early. CRM, follow-up processes, and proposal templates saved time. Fourth, they focused on their competitive advantage (high-quality large projects) instead of trying to do everything. Fifth, they built referral systems before they had satisfied customers to leverage.

The 1500% ROI came from combining low marketing spend ($30K), high-value deals ($35K average), and strong execution. This formula works particularly well for boutique restoration companies with premium positioning.

What This Teaches Other Companies

Not all restoration companies need volume. Some are better positioned for premium work. If that's your strength, focus your marketing on attracting premium opportunities. Precision beats volume. Higher margins beat higher transactions. Satisfied customers who refer beats constant new customer acquisition.

Sunshine proved that a small team with strong marketing can compete against larger companies. Growth doesn't require franchise systems or massive teams. It requires clear positioning, laser-focused marketing, and excellent execution.
