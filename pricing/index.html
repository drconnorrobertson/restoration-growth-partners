Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade packages? ▼

Yes. You can change packages anytime based on your needs and budget. If you want to scale, we scale. If you need to adjust down, we adjust.

Do you require long-term contracts? ▼

No. We work on month-to-month agreements. You can stop anytime, but we work to earn your business every month.

Are there any setup fees? ▼

We sometimes have a one-time setup fee ($500-$1,500) if we're building a new website or doing extensive technical SEO work. We discuss this upfront before starting.

Is the ad spend included in your pricing? ▼

No. The Growth System and Scale packages include management of ads but not the ad spend itself. If we're running Google Ads with a $2K/month budget, that's $2K on top of your package cost.

What if I'm not happy with results? ▼

We work together to improve results. We track everything to ROI and adjust strategy based on performance. If we're not generating results after a fair trial period, you can cancel without penalty.
