Why Angi's List and 33Mile Radius Are Killing Your Profit Margins

The Math Behind Marketplace Economics and How to Escape the Margin Compression Trap

Published April 28, 2026 | 10 min read

Let's do the math on marketplace platforms like Angi's List and 33Mile Radius.

You pay $50-$100/month for a premium account. Then, for every lead that comes through the platform, you pay either:

  • A percentage of the job (usually 20-35%)
  • A flat fee per lead ($50-$200)
  • Or a combination of both

Let's say you're a water damage restoration company in Denver. Your average job is $8,000. You pay Angi's 25% commission. That's $2,000 per job going to Angi's. After the job, your profit margin drops from 35% to roughly 10% because of the commission.

Worse, you're competing against 5-10 other companies bidding on the same job. Homeowners see all of you and naturally pick the cheapest option. The lead generates volume but kills margins.

This is the marketplace trap. The platforms promise volume. They deliver it. But the volume comes with margin compression that kills profitability.

The Deeper Problem With Marketplaces

It's not just about commissions. There are deeper structural problems:

You Don't Own the Customer Relationship - The platform owns the customer. They own the phone number, email, feedback, and history. When the customer needs more work, they go back to the platform, not to you directly. You're interchangeable.

You Have No Leverage - The platform controls pricing, visibility, and lead distribution. They can increase commission rates anytime. They can bury you in search results. You have no recourse.

Algorithm Risk - Platforms change their algorithms. One change and your visibility drops 50%. Your lead volume disappears overnight. You have no control.

Race to the Bottom on Price - With 10 companies bidding on every job, customers always pick the cheapest. This forces you to cut prices or lose jobs. Your margins shrink every year.

The Math on Building Your Own Lead Sources

Now let's look at the alternative: building your own lead sources through Google Ads, SEO, and organic channels.

Scenario 1: Google Ads Campaign

  • Monthly budget: $2,000
  • Average cost per lead: $40
  • Leads per month: 50
  • Conversion rate: 20% (average for restoration companies)
  • Jobs per month: 10
  • Revenue per month: $80,000
  • Cost per job: $200
  • Margin impact: 2.5% (vs. 25% for marketplace commissions)

You're spending the same $2,000 on Google Ads that you'd spend on Angi's, but you're getting 10 jobs with only 2.5% commission equivalent vs. 25% for the marketplace.

Scenario 2: SEO (Long-term asset)

  • Monthly investment in SEO: $2,000
  • Time to results: 3-6 months
  • Once ranking: 15-20 organic leads per month (free)
  • Long-term margin impact: 0% (no commission)

With SEO, you invest for 6 months and then generate free leads indefinitely. Your margin impact is zero. You own the traffic. Google can't take it away (unless you stop optimizing).

The Real Cost of Marketplace Dependency

Here's what marketplace dependency costs you over a year:

  • 50 jobs per year from marketplaces
  • $400,000 revenue per year
  • $100,000 in marketplace commissions per year
  • Effective profit margin: 10% instead of 35%
  • Lost profit: $100,000+ per year

That $100,000 is money you could be investing in your business, paying your team, or taking home as profit.

How to Escape the Marketplace Trap

Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Build Foundation

  • Optimize Google Business Profile
  • Build review generation system
  • Create location-specific landing pages
  • Fix technical SEO on your website

Investment: $1,500-$3,000

Phase 2 (Months 3-6): Generate Immediate Leads

  • Launch Google Ads campaigns
  • Launch Google Local Services Ads
  • Create partnership with insurance adjusters
  • Start referral program with customers

Investment: $2,000-$4,000/month

Phase 3 (Months 6-12): Build Organic Assets

  • Implement SEO strategy
  • Create blog content
  • Build backlinks
  • Optimize for long-tail keywords

Investment: $1,500-$3,000/month

By month 12, you have:

  • Stable Google Ads leads
  • Strong organic traffic starting to convert
  • Referral relationships generating consistent volume
  • Multiple lead sources reducing marketplace dependency

Your commission equivalent drops from 25% to 5-10% (mostly from the remaining paid ads). Your margins recover to 25-30%.

Real Example: A Water Damage Company's Transition

One company we worked with was paying Angi's and 33Mile $1,500/month and getting 20 jobs/month with low margins. After 12 months of building their own lead system:

  • Month 1-3: Built GBP, citations, website foundation
  • Month 4-6: Launched Google Ads (got 15 leads/month), started referral system
  • Month 6-12: Built SEO, added content, optimized conversion
  • Month 12 result: Getting 25 leads/month from owned channels, reduced Angi's to $500/month

By month 12, they were getting more leads with better margins. By month 18, they eliminated marketplace dependency entirely and were getting 30+ leads/month from owned channels.

The Path Forward

You don't have to quit marketplaces immediately. But you should start building alternative lead sources right now. The longer you're dependent on marketplaces, the longer your margins are compressed.

The restoration companies that are thriving aren't the ones paying the highest commissions. They're the ones that took control of their lead generation and built systems they own.
