The Restoration Growth System

Our proven process for helping restoration companies build predictable, profitable lead generation systems.

The 6-Phase System

1

Discovery & Assessment

We analyze your current situation, lead sources, conversion rates, and market opportunity. We understand where you are and what's possible for your specific market and service areas.

2

Strategy & Roadmap

We create a custom 12-month growth roadmap. This includes which channels to prioritize, expected timelines, realistic projections, and investment levels. You know exactly what we're going to do and what to expect.

3

Foundation Building

We build your foundation: optimize Google Business Profile, fix technical SEO, create your website if needed, build local citations. This is the groundwork that makes everything else work.

4

Immediate Lead Generation

We launch paid campaigns (Google Ads, Local Services Ads) to generate immediate qualified leads while organic assets build in the background. You get revenue flowing quickly.

5

Long-Term Asset Building

We implement SEO, content marketing, review generation, and relationship building. These assets compound over time and create free leads that keep flowing for years.

6

Scale & Optimization

As results come in, we scale what works and optimize continuously. You get monthly reporting showing exactly where leads come from and what they cost vs. revenue.

Month-by-Month Timeline

Months 1-3: Foundation

  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Website audit and optimization
  • Local citations built and corrected
  • Review generation system implemented
  • Keyword research and strategy
  • Early paid ad setup and testing

Months 4-6: Initial Results

  • First qualified leads from Google Ads and Local Services Ads
  • GBP ranking improving in local pack
  • SEO work beginning (content creation, technical optimization)
  • Review count increasing
  • Lead tracking and ROI analysis
  • Ad optimization based on performance

Months 7-12: Scaling & Compounding

  • Paid ads scaling based on positive ROI
  • Organic SEO results starting to compound
  • Dominating Google Maps local pack
  • Building location-specific content
  • Partnership and referral relationships strengthening
  • Overall lead volume 2-3X higher than month 1

What Makes Our Process Different

Data-Driven

Every decision is based on data. We track leads, conversions, and revenue. We adjust based on what's working, not guesses or trends.

Dual-Channel Strategy

We combine paid ads (immediate leads) with organic assets (long-term leads). You get revenue quickly and build sustainable assets.

ROI Focused

We measure everything in terms of revenue generated. Not clicks, not impressions, not rankings. Real revenue from real leads.

Transparent Communication

Monthly reporting shows exactly where leads come from, cost, and revenue. You understand the system and see the value.

Continuous Optimization

We don't set it and forget it. Every month we review performance, test new approaches, and scale what works.

Partnership Mentality

We're invested in your success. We win when you win. Long-term thinking, not short-term commissions.
