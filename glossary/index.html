Restoration Marketing Glossary

Essential terminology for understanding restoration company digital marketing

Use this glossary to understand key restoration marketing terms and concepts. Each term includes a detailed definition, relevance to restoration companies, and practical examples.

Glossary Terms

Cost Per Lead (CPL)

The average amount spent on marketing to acquire each lead. Calculated by dividing total marketing spend by number of leads generated.

Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

The total cost spent to acquire a single paying customer. Includes all marketing and sales expenses divided by number of customers acquired.

Local SEO

Optimization techniques to improve visibility in local search results for geographic-specific searches.

Google Business Profile (GBP)

Free Google listing that displays business information including location, hours, reviews, and services.

Pay Per Click (PPC)

Digital advertising model where you pay each time someone clicks on your ad.

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Techniques to improve website visibility in organic (non-paid) search results.

Conversion Rate

Percentage of website visitors who complete a desired action, such as filling out a form or calling.

Bounce Rate

Percentage of website visitors who leave without taking any action or visiting another page.

Click Through Rate (CTR)

Percentage of people who click on your ad after seeing it. Calculated as clicks divided by impressions.

Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

Revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising. Calculated by dividing revenue by ad spend.

Lead Scoring

System for ranking leads based on their likelihood to convert into customers.

Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL)

Lead that has shown interest in your services and meets certain criteria for sales follow-up.

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Total profit generated from a customer relationship over its entire duration.

Organic Traffic

Website visitors that arrive through unpaid search results.

Paid Traffic

Website visitors acquired through paid advertising (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, etc.).

Backlinks

Links from other websites to your website. Important for SEO authority.

Domain Authority (DA)

Measure of how authoritative your website is, based on backlinks and other factors.

Keyword Research

Process of identifying search terms that potential customers use when looking for your services.

Landing Page

Standalone web page designed to convert visitors into leads or customers.

Call to Action (CTA)

Instruction to encourage visitors to take a specific action, like filling a form or calling.

Retargeting

Advertising strategy to show ads to people who visited your website but didn't convert.

Content Marketing

Creating and distributing valuable content to attract and engage potential customers.

Reputation Management

Process of monitoring and improving online reviews and brand perception.

Local Citations

Online mentions of your business name, address, and phone number on directories and websites.
