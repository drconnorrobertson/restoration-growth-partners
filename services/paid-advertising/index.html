Our Google Ads Management System

We've built a proven process for managing restoration company PPC campaigns at scale.

1. Keyword Research and Strategy

We identify high-intent keywords your customers are searching for: "water damage restoration," "emergency mold removal," "fire damage repair," and location-specific variations. We prioritize keywords with high conversion intent and reasonable costs.

2. Campaign Structure

We organize campaigns by service type (water damage, mold, fire restoration), geography, and funnel stage. This structure allows us to bid strategically, control costs, and optimize for different conversion types across your market.

3. Ad Copy Optimization

Your ads tell a compelling story. We craft ad copy that addresses pain points, highlights your expertise, and makes the call-to-action clear. We test variations constantly to improve click-through rates.

4. Landing Page Strategy

Traffic is worthless if landing pages don't convert. We ensure ads lead to highly relevant landing pages optimized for each service and location. We continuously test and improve conversion rates.

5. Bid Management

We use Google's Smart Bidding and manual bid strategies to control costs and maximize conversions. We monitor CPC, CPA, and ROAS constantly, adjusting bids to stay profitable.

6. Continuous Optimization

We analyze performance data weekly, looking for opportunities to improve CTR, lower costs, and increase conversions. Every campaign element is tested and refined.
