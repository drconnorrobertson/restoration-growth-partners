# Water damage restoration marketing search plan

Prepared 2026-09-22 for Restoration Growth Partners.

## Goal and measurement

Primary business goal: qualified water damage restoration opportunities and booked jobs from nonbranded organic search. Search visibility is a leading indicator, not the final outcome. Establish the current baseline in Google Search Console and the CRM before judging results.

The user-supplied keyword screenshot reports these volume and KD estimates: "water damage restoration marketing" 390 / 14, "water restoration marketing" 390 / 18, "marketing for restoration companies" 320 / 11, "restoration marketing" 720 / 20, and "restoration market" 590 / 19. These are third-party estimates from an unidentified tool and date. Verify geography, match type, and current values before forecasting. "Restoration market" is ambiguous and should not receive a dedicated service page without confirming intent.

## Internal-link pattern

Homepage and services index -> water damage service page.
Water damage service page -> guide, channel pages, relevant case studies, contact.
Guide -> service page and each spoke.
Spokes -> guide and relevant service page.
Existing lead-generation and Google Ads pages -> new hub and guide.

Use descriptive anchors in context. Avoid repeating exact-match anchors across every page.

## Editorial standards

Every page should answer an actual buyer or operator question, reflect RGP's real offer, and contain examples or proof that can be verified. Replace generic statements with documented processes, screenshots, original data, or client-approved case studies. Avoid invented statistics, guaranteed rankings, and unsubstantiated outcome claims. Review insurance, certification, and response-time statements with the business before publication.

## Technical release checklist

- Confirm canonical URLs resolve to the intended 200 response on the deployed Vercel site.
- Check title, description, H1, structured data, navigation, and mobile layout.
- Add new URLs to the sitemap and submit the sitemap in Search Console.
- Inspect whether the old guide is indexed and whether it earns queries before changing its URL. This implementation keeps its existing URL.
- Verify internal links and the contact flow.
- Check the site for duplicate or thin city pages separately; do not scale location templates simply to increase URL count.

## 90-day sequence

Weeks 1-2: benchmark impressions, clicks, average position, organic qualified inquiries, booked jobs, and revenue; QA and release this cluster.
Weeks 3-4: add verified client proof and original visual examples to hub and guide. Improve the six existing channel service pages.
Weeks 5-8: refresh the existing lead generation and Google Ads articles using actual RGP insights; publish a useful budget worksheet or calculator; earn relevant industry coverage.
Weeks 9-12: assess query-to-page fit in Search Console. Improve pages with impressions but weak CTR or engagement. Expand only into distinct questions surfaced by real search and sales data.

## One-site strategy

Build and maintain this campaign on restorationgrowthpartners.com. Publish future research, calculators, templates, and case studies in the most relevant sections of the same site. Do not create satellite domains for these keywords. Keep one clear URL for each search intent and consolidate overlapping pages as the library grows.

## Reporting

Track weekly: target-query impressions/clicks/position, indexed URL status, and organic qualified leads. Track monthly: calls answered, qualified opportunities, booked jobs, revenue, cost per booked job, and source confidence. Compare all metrics to the prelaunch baseline; do not promise page-one rankings.
