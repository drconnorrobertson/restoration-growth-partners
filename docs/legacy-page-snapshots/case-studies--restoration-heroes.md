Restoration Heroes
How Restoration Heroes Achieved $300K+ in Revenue Generated in 3 Months with Restoration Growth Partners
Restoration Heroes is based in Irvine, CA. They specialize in high-end residential & commercial clientele. When they came to RGP, they didn't have a solid web presence. In the last 3 months with us, they have done:
$300,000+ in Revenue Generated in 3 Months
1,300% Return on Investment
Average Cost Per Job: $145
Learn more about it
The Problem
Restoration Heroes was growing YoY and building a solid brand. While they were attracting their ideal clientele, they too were relying on real-world marketing methods in order to get jobs.
When they came to RGP, they knew that in order to dominate their area and leapfrog the competition, digital marketing would be at the core of it.
Unfortunately, they had tried other marketing companies in the past, who promised to do "SEO", but they saw no results from it.
How Did We Fix This?
The Solution
"Blended Search" approach for the competitive area of Orange County
Insight & Discovery
Performed a Insight & Discovery analysis and found a ton of areas to improve.
Laser Focused SEO
SEO Campaign that focused on the top performing cities, areas, and services in order to generate the highest amount of revenue possible.
We doubled down on the cities that historically produced them the highest amount of revenue, then moved onto cities that had the highest search volume.
Google Business Profile
Daily optimization of the Google Business Profile, by generating reviews, posts, service categories, pictures, and more. Generated an additional 25 reviews.
We are now opening a 2nd GBP with them, in order to expand their service radius.
Quality Content
Filled their website with high-quality content that users and Google love, increasing their rankings.
Citations & Backlinks
Utilizing our pre-made & vetted list of the best citations and backlinks to acquire, we threw "fuel on the fire" for this campaign, massively increasing rankings.
