Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about restoration marketing, lead generation, and our services.

General Questions

How long does it take to see results? ▼

Paid advertising campaigns generate leads within 2-4 weeks. SEO and organic results take longer (typically 3-6 months to see significant traction), but these are assets that keep generating leads for years without ongoing ad spend. The best approach is combining both for immediate results while building long-term assets.

How much does this cost? ▼

Our pricing depends on your specific needs, market size, and goals. We offer several packages ranging from foundational SEO and GBP optimization to full-service systems including paid advertising, content, and reputation management. Most clients invest $3K-$10K per month. We discuss your situation and budget before proposing anything. See our pricing page for details.

Do you work with all types of restoration companies? ▼

Yes. We specialize in water damage, mold remediation, fire restoration, and general restoration. We also work with carpet cleaning, air duct cleaning, and other related services. The principles are the same: create authority in search, generate leads, track ROI.

What if we're already using Angi's List or 33Mile Radius? ▼

That's exactly who we work with. Most of our clients start with us while still using marketplaces, then gradually shift marketing budget away from high-margin platforms toward owned channels. We help you build diversified lead sources so you're not dependent on any single platform.

Services & Strategy

What's the difference between SEO and paid advertising? ▼

SEO is organic traffic from search engines. It takes time to build but generates leads indefinitely once you rank. Paid advertising generates immediate leads but costs money on an ongoing basis. The best approach combines both: paid ads for immediate revenue while SEO builds long-term assets.

How important is Google Business Profile? ▼

Very important. Google Business Profile signals account for nearly a third of local search ranking factors. If you're not showing up in the Google Maps pack where customers search in emergencies, you're losing thousands in potential revenue. Optimizing your GBP is often the highest-impact quick win.

Do you require long-term contracts? ▼

No. We work on month-to-month agreements. You can stop anytime, but we earn your business every month by delivering results.

How do you track ROI? ▼

We implement conversion tracking on all channels (phone calls, form fills, chat), integrate with your CRM if possible, and track leads back to revenue. You see exactly what each channel costs and what it generates. No vanity metrics, just revenue.

Implementation & Support

How much involvement do I need to have? ▼

Minimal. We handle most of the marketing work. You need to: approve strategy, provide feedback on direction, and make sure we have access to your CRM and customer data for tracking. We handle the rest.

What if my website is outdated or non-existent? ▼

We can build or rebuild your website as part of the engagement. This is usually a one-time project separate from ongoing marketing. We specialize in fast, conversion-optimized websites built specifically for restoration companies.

How often do I hear from you? ▼

You get monthly reporting with all performance metrics. Most clients also have a monthly call with their account manager to review results and adjust strategy. For urgent questions or issues, we're available via email or phone.

Can you integrate with my CRM? ▼

Yes. We integrate with most major CRM systems (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Jobber, etc.). This helps us track leads and attribute them properly so you see ROI by channel.

Results & Guarantees

What kind of results can I expect? ▼

Our clients typically see 280-520% increases in qualified leads within 12 months. Some see results faster (particularly paid ads), some take longer (particularly SEO). It depends on your market, competition, and starting point. We'll give you realistic expectations during your consultation.

Do you guarantee results? ▼

We can't guarantee specific results because markets vary, competition varies, and execution matters. But we guarantee we'll give you our best effort, track everything to ROI, and be honest about what's working. If results aren't happening after a fair trial, we discuss options including adjusting strategy or ending the engagement.

What if I'm not happy after a few months? ▼

We work with you to improve results or adjust strategy. Give us time to make changes (typically 3-6 months depending on the channel). If we're still not delivering after a fair trial period, you can cancel without penalty.
