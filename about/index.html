About Restoration Growth Partners

We help restoration companies escape marketplace dependency and build predictable, profitable growth systems.

Our Mission

To help restoration companies break free from reliance on expensive marketplaces and build sustainable growth systems that generate qualified leads they own and control. We believe restoration companies should keep more of their revenue, have more control over their customer relationships, and have predictable growth.

What We Do

We build complete digital marketing systems for restoration companies. This includes SEO, paid advertising, Google Business Profile optimization, website design, reputation management, and content marketing. Everything is tied to actual revenue generated.

Who We Serve

Water damage restoration companies, mold remediation businesses, fire restoration services, and general restoration companies. We work with companies from solo operations to multi-location franchises.

Our Guarantee

We track everything to revenue. Every dollar spent is measured against leads generated and revenue produced. If it's not working, we change it. We're aligned with your success.

Our Experience

We've worked with 40+ restoration companies across 20+ states. We've generated over $8M in revenue for our clients through lead generation and growth systems.

Why We Do This

We started Restoration Growth Partners after working with dozens of restoration companies and seeing the same problem repeatedly: they were profitable, but paying 20-40% of their revenue to marketplace platforms. They wanted more control. They wanted better margins. They wanted predictable growth.

We realized there was a better way. By combining paid advertising (for immediate leads) with organic strategies like SEO and Google Business Profile optimization (for long-term assets), restoration companies could cut marketplace dependency, improve margins, and generate leads predictably.

That's what we do. We help restoration companies build the systems that let them win in their markets without being dependent on any single platform.
